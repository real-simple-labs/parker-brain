## First candidate content — and what is still missing

A strategic pattern is queued for this doc but no prompting method has been captured yet, so the placeholder stands. Watch entry 8 in `parker-taste/patterns-to-monitor/INDEX.md` describes trigger-event animation: AI-animated ads, often carrying a song, that open on the moment a problem became undeniable rather than on the problem itself. The reason it is not written here is precise — the source described *what the format does and why it works*, and said nothing about how to prompt it. The hook mechanic it depends on is already canon at `hooks.md` #22.

What this doc still needs before it can be written honestly: how character consistency is held across shots, how a staged two-character moment is specified, how sung or musical voiceover is directed, and where animation prompting genuinely diverges from `veo3-video-prompting.md` rather than restating it. Those come from real runs, not from a description of finished ads. Do not fill this doc from ad observation alone — a prompting method inferred from watching outputs is the fabrication this repo's attribution rules exist to prevent.
