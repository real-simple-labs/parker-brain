# Expert signal - Meta Creative Shop, "Creative on Meta: Creative Playbook"

## Source read

A 50-page PDF from Meta's Creative Shop, handed to Parker by the factory team on 2026-10-09 with a request to add it as global context for advertising on Meta in 2026. Read in full via text extraction. Embedded example videos and mockup imagery were not viewable; their captions and labels were. Linked companion playbooks, the motivator-map downloads, and the worksheet download were not read.

## What it teaches

Meta's case for creative diversification at the ad-set level (formats, visuals, messages) feeding impression-level personalization; a three-format minimum per ad set with stated CPA and incremental-conversion lifts for including 9:16 video with audio at 20%+ of assets; the Reels creative essentials and five "language of Reels" elements; static-ad rules; the motivators concepting method with a motivator-to-concept chain, white-space maps, and 11 vertical motivator maps; creator-squad diversity and input-based creator briefs; remixing and format adaptation for asset volume; and an inventory of Advantage+ creative generative-AI features with stated lifts.

## Decision

New knowledge doc rather than an edit to `andromeda-v2.md`. The Andromeda doc is practitioner doctrine on delivery mechanics; this is the platform's first-party view with its own numbers and its own concepting method, and it disagrees with Andromeda in three named places (hook-swap remixes, message-only variants, one-variable iteration). Keeping them separate preserves the attribution and lets the tension be stated rather than blended. Routed from the own-account read, the diversity audit, concepting and briefs, and iteration reads. Cross-linked from `andromeda-v2.md`.

Not added: the downstream craft docs (`hooks.md`, `static-ad-design.md`, `creator-briefs.md`, `persona-research-and-creative-strategy-process.md`) already hold deeper versions of the overlapping guidance; the new doc points to them instead of duplicating into them.

## Idea-bank decision

No idea-bank entry. The playbook's examples are illustrative mockups for unnamed or fictional brands, not reusable ads.

## Source limits

All figures are stated by Meta. Several studies are limited to large advertisers, several are observational models, one key figure is at 70% confidence, and the creative-element lift studies date to 2023. The 2% Facebook Reels conversion lift is not clearly tied to a specific feature in the source.
