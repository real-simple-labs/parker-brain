# Personas

This README explains the folder. It is not one of the folder's docs, so leave it out of any list or index of them.

Who buys, why they buy, and the exact words they use.

## What lives here

- The persona profiles, with the lifecycle journey maps, the persona voice library and the cross-persona bias notes.
- `voice-of-customer/`: the customer's own language. `voice-of-customer.md` is the assembled read, `voc-corpus-profile.md` describes the corpus behind it, and one `voc-[category].md` per kind of phrase: pain, outcome, objection, trigger moment, aspiration, metaphor, category jargon, anti-language, surprise and delight.

The prompts that write these docs are in `parker-system/prompts/personas/` and `parker-system/prompts/voice-of-customer/`. They build on the raw reads in `../source-pulls/`. The method is in `parker-system/creative-strategy-context/persona-research-and-creative-strategy-process.md` and `customer-review-mining-method.md`.

A new brain starts with this folder empty. The build fills it.
