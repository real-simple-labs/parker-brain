# Sprints

This README explains the folder. It is not one of the folder's docs, so leave it out of any list or index of them.

One folder per creative round: what the round makes, the briefs for it, and what it taught.

## What lives here

- `[YYYY-MM-DD]-[sprint-slug]/sprint-plan.md`: the sized round and its concept map.
- `[YYYY-MM-DD]-[sprint-slug]/briefs/`: one brief per concept, with its variations and creator direction.
- `[YYYY-MM-DD]-[sprint-slug]/retro.md`: what the round's results said, which sizes the next round.
- `_unplanned/briefs/`: briefs written on request, with no planned round behind them.

The prompts are `parker-system/prompts/ideas-and-briefs/sprint-plan.md` and `brief-creation.md`. How to brief a creator is in `parker-system/creative-strategy-context/creator-briefs.md`.

A new brain starts with this folder empty. The build fills it in Phase 3.
