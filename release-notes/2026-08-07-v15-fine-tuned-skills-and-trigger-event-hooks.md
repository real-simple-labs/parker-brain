# v15 — fine-tuned skills, the trigger-event hook, and webinars in the brain

Promoted out of the 2026-08-06 monthly webinar, which also becomes the first entry in a new standing surface — joined, later the same day, by the backfilled 2026-07-02 session.

## Fine-tuned skills — a brain grows downward, not just sideways

`system/growing-the-brain.md` covered growing the brain *sideways*: new domains, new surfaces, new kinds of truth as the org connects more of itself. It said nothing about growing it *downward*, into doing the craft it already does better. That gap was load-bearing, because the pattern was already being taught to customers and the factory had no method for it.

A **fine-tuned skill** is a brand-authored sibling of a factory skill, built from the team's own corpus — the three hundred scripts in their Notion, the iterations they've run, the briefs they've written, or a client channel's entire feedback history, which is a corpus about taste rather than output. The factory craft skills are general on purpose; a team with a real body of work holds the thing the method cannot: how *they* do it.

The mechanics that make it work:

- **Delta, not fork.** The fine-tune references the factory skill and states what is different here. A full copy with a few lines changed stops receiving factory improvements and hides what was actually brand-specific.
- **Profile docs first, skill second.** A corpus read produces a script-voice profile and a visual vocabulary before it produces a skill; those are brand docs, and the skill sits on top encoding process differences.
- **A distinct name is the whole trick.** `<brand>-scriptwriting`, never `scriptwriting`. `scripts/sync-executable-layer.py` refreshes the copied layer against a bundle map of factory skill names; a name outside that map is invisible to the sync and survives every pin bump untouched. This is why a fine-tuned sibling beats editing a copied skill in place — the edited copy freezes at the team's version while the factory's moves on, and the sibling doesn't.
- **Provenance and a refresh cadence.** How many pieces, from which surface, over what window. And a fine-tune built on last year's work quietly teaches the brain to write like the brand used to, so it goes on `running-notes/refresh-schedule.md` like any standing doc.

This is the missing third rung of the adaptation ladder in the brand `CLAUDE.md`: brand docs first, fine-tuned sibling when it's a real process difference with a corpus behind it, editing a copy in place only when neither works.

`templates/brand-brain-CLAUDE-template.md` carries the runtime half — the precedence rule (where a fine-tuned skill exists it is the default for that craft, with the factory skill as the method underneath and the fallback) and a "## The map" line so the planning pass can see what exists. A fine-tuned skill nobody routes to is the same as no fine-tune at all.

**Ships with real-step `migrations/v15.md`**, because the brain's root `CLAUDE.md` is brand-authored and sits outside both the mount and the sync bundle. The migration touches only that file and creates no skills — building a fine-tune is the team's call.

## Trigger-event hooks

`hooks.md` gains **#22 Trigger-Event**: open on the single concrete moment the problem stopped being private and became undeniable, almost always because another person registered it — a child's question, a relative's remark, a partner's reaction. Not the condition, not the journey.

Two distinctions the doc makes explicitly, because both are easy to blur:

- **vs #14 Trigger *Word***, which is about charged vocabulary and unrelated.
- **vs #18 Storytelling**, which opens a journey and pays off with an arc. A trigger event detonates in a single beat and promises nothing; the recognition *is* the payoff. Storytelling earns attention with narrative pull, this earns it with exposure.

`customer-review-mining-method.md` gains the matching qualifying signal, which is what makes the hook usable — these moments have to be sourced from customers, never invented, and most mining passes walk straight past them because they read as background to a review rather than its point. The entry names how to spot one (a named other person, a specific occasion, a time-stamped beat inside an otherwise general review) and carries two governors harder than usual: a single striking moment isn't a pattern until the *kind* of moment recurs, and health, body, and appearance moments carry claims and personal-attribute constraints downstream.

The hardcoded hook-format list in `analyzing-public-ad-accounts.md` was resynced — it had also silently drifted past #21.

**The format also got its execution layer, which is a separate claim from the taxonomy having it.** The hooks skill carries a per-format process file for each format family, and `processes/INDEX.md` reasons explicitly about which formats deliberately lack one — so a new format sitting silently absent reads as an oversight. New `processes/trigger-event.md` holds the writing playbook (source the moment, stage one scene, put the witness in frame, keep the line the customer's, resolve it), with the cold-traffic zone caution carried in because this is a high-intensity open. It is routed from three places in `strategy.md`: the format menu, the problem-aware awareness line, and a new **shame or social-cost** emotional driver — none of the five existing drivers covered a problem whose real stakes are dignity rather than function. A process file nothing routes to is the same as no process file.

Separately, the **testimonial-trap** rule was qualified in the skill layer to match the doc. `lifestyle-headline-generator.md` now distinguishes praise (which does not scale) from a verbatim carrying identity, a vivid physical image, or real emotional charge (which does, and gets lifted in her register rather than sanded). Three skill files restated the trap flatly and now carry the exception: `headlines/SKILL.md`, `headlines/strategy.md`, and `iterations/processes/static-headline-iteration.md` — the last found only by grepping siblings, and already drifted a step further with a phrase the headlines version never had. `headlines/processes/headline-from-review-verbatim.md` was deliberately left alone; it already drew the distinction correctly, and the doc change ratified it rather than changing it.

## What deliberately did not become canon

The format the hook was observed inside — AI animation, often carrying a song — went to `parker-taste/patterns-to-monitor/` as **entry 8**, not into the format taxonomy. One operator, no account data, evidence offered as impression rank rather than performance, and three confounded variables (animation, trigger-open, song) that no example separates. The entry names the pull that would promote it.

`ai-animation-prompting.md` **stays a placeholder**, with the candidate content and the missing pieces named. The source described what the format does and why it works, and said nothing about how to prompt it; a prompting method inferred from watching finished ads is exactly the fabrication the attribution rules exist to prevent.

Watch **entry 6** (purchase-trigger capture) was **partially promoted**: its research half is now corroborated and lives in the review-mining method, while the checkout-survey capture mechanism stays on the list. The two triggers are separated explicitly so they don't collapse into each other — the problem trigger is when tolerating it stopped being possible, the purchase trigger is when the cost of inaction exceeded the price, and a customer can sit between them for months.

## Impression rank, scoped

From a maintainer correction. `analyzing-public-ad-accounts.md` now states it unconditionally at the top: **impression rank is a substitute for data you don't have, never a supplement to data you do.** Every proxy in that doc exists because the account's real numbers are unreachable, and the moment Parker has account access they're obsolete — spend, ROAS, CPA, and the fatigue curve say what an ad *did*, while impression rank only says the brand kept paying for delivery.

So: right for competitors, inspo brands, and affinity brands; wrong for the brand's own account or any client account the team can see, which goes to `ad-account-analysis.md`. One narrow exception is reading the brand's own public library deliberately as an outsider would, which is a positioning read and not a performance one. Repeated at point of use in the step-five lens list and carried into `best-use-cases.md`.

## Webinars become a standing surface

New `global/knowledge/webinars/` — Parker's own monthly customer webinars, each pairing a dense digest with the verbatim transcript, so a user can ask what was announced, demoed, or answered on a given call. First-party sessions only; third-party expert transcripts keep routing through `expert-insights/`. `README.md`'s "raw transcripts" exclusion carries the carve-out explicitly. Two standing read cautions on every file: product state is dated to the call, and figures the hosts say live are `stated`.

`global/knowledge/best-use-cases.md` gained nine use cases from the session — discovery-database swipe files and briefs, Reddit, affinity-brand follow-through, cross-client agency analysis, fine-tuning a skill on your own back catalog, feeding the brain outside content, the hypothesis-tracker routine, and browser control (Claude sees the ad library you're scrolling, so research becomes pointing rather than describing) — plus a working-habits section and about seventeen new prompts. The transcript stays as the receipt.

## The 2026-07-02 session, backfilled — and connect your tools before the build

The July session went into the same folder the same day. It's five weeks older, so its product state is historical — Discovery, Discover Brands, Northbeam and post-purchase surveys were all beta or unshipped on the call and had landed by August — and the file carries a supersession note plus a table of what moved. Same rule as the index: where two sessions disagree, the newer one wins.

**Almost none of it was new.** The swipe file, affinity brands, impression-rank reads, reporting and competitor-digest and static-generation prompts, Notion idea-library population, following brands from the chat, TikTok mining, routines, dreaming, and the correct-it-once pattern were already canon — several of them promoted from the August session five weeks later, where the same operator said them again more fully. The digest says that plainly rather than padding a contribution list. Five things genuinely weren't there:

- **Connect your own tools before the build, not after.** The heaviest read Parker ever runs on a brand is the build itself, so a tool that's live while it runs gets read *into* the foundation, while one connected afterwards has to be reconciled against a V0 already written without it. The build sequence had the Parker MCP as a Phase 0 gate and pushed the team's own tools — Notion, Slack, Drive — to `/get-started` at the end. It now has a short pre-Phase-0 step: ask what the team works in, wire it up, log what they name but don't connect. Explicitly **not** a gate; the Parker MCP is still the only connection the build needs. Lands in `system/parker-tools.md` (the standing rule), `prompts/onboarding-runner.md`, `.claude/skills/set-up-brain/SKILL.md`, and `best-use-cases.md`.
- **A yardstick for the intake's closing gap question.** "Anything else I should know?" reliably gets a one-line answer. The framing that fixes it: if you're a brand, say everything you'd want an agency you just hired to know; if you're an agency, everything you'd tell a new strategist joining the account. Dictate it, be long rather than tidy.
- **Audit your own week.** Every use case in `best-use-cases.md` pointed Parker at the brand's data; none pointed it at the user's own working process. New section and prompt: describe your week out loud, then ask what can be taken off your plate given what's actually connected. Named on the call as the source of most of the session's other prompts.
- **A reviewer *after* generation, not just judges before it.** The existing judges pattern scores ideas pre-generation. This is the other half — a second agent critiquing generated output and regenerating against its own feedback, which is the cheapest fix for a batch that lands at "close, but the copy's weak."
- **Two Parker MCP tools missing from the canonical inventory.** `search_swipe_file` (org-scoped, not brand-scoped, and it's what the Chrome extension and every save button write into) and `brand_discovery` (ranks external brands on audience, positioning and tone, and is what populates the affinity set). Both back the session's headline demos; both verified present in the live toolset and added to `system/parker-tools.md`.

**And the Reddit gap is closed.** `system/parker-tools.md` had a standing "flag this for eng" section stating Parker has **no native reach into Reddit or any forum**. That was true when written and stopped being true when Reddit shipped — it was in limited testing on 6 August and is live now. `search_reddit_posts_and_comments` joins the inventory: the brand's own indexed posts and comments as flat peers, from curated subreddits scraped on a schedule, gated on that brand completing Reddit onboarding, with the semantic leg taking 4–10 short keyword phrases rather than a sentence. The web-search section keeps its real constraint — there is still no open-web search, and forums other than Reddit are still unreachable — but now says to **check Reddit before declaring a community question `data-limited`**, because a good share of what used to land in that bucket is answerable. The stale claim appeared in exactly one place; nothing else carried it.

Deliberately not promoted: the Fable 5 build recommendation (a point-in-time model call, not repeated in August), the Higgsfield local-assets limitation (contested live, never tested, still open), and the roadmap items. **No new watch entry** — the session made no craft-method claim that isn't already canon, so `parker-taste/patterns-to-monitor/` is untouched.

**A coverage audit closed three more gaps.** Reading `best-use-cases.md` back against *both* transcripts caught two use cases the first promotion missed, both from the 2026-08-06 session: **extending the brain past paid creative** into the other channels a team runs — growth, organic, CRO and landing pages, email and SMS — by describing the structure and letting it propose the shape rather than designing folders (`system/growing-the-brain.md` had the method, but nothing told a *user* this was possible, and it's one of the biggest answers to "what can I do with this"); and **browsing the discovery database with the format filter off**, which is the second of the two ways the Discovery tab was demoed — the "what should we make?" direction rather than "find me more of this," read as a diff against your own library. Plus one from July: routines are set up **by saying so in the chat**, not in a settings screen, which is worth stating because assuming there's a proper place to go is the most common reason people never automate anything. The 2026-08-06 file's promotion list now records items 10 and 11 so the receipts match.

**No migration steps.** Everything here either rides the mount or sits inside the sync script's bundle map, so the `v15` pin bump delivers all of it. `migrations/v15.md` is unchanged and still covers only the fine-tuned-skill precedence rule, which is the one piece that touches brand-authored content.
